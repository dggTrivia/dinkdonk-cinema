DinkDonk Cinema

Checking your link…

1 · Connect to OBS

  1. In OBS: Tools › WebSocket Server Settings › tick Enable WebSocket server. Click Show Connect Info for the password.
  2. For the film on the cinema screen, install the obs-shaderfilter plugin. Without it, the screen stays green and you place the film yourself.
  3. Open this page on the PC that runs OBS. If your browser asks to allow access to devices on your local network, allow it: that's how this page reaches OBS.

2 · Create the scenes

This adds three scenes to your current scene collection: DinkDonk Lobby, DinkDonk Cinema (the auditorium) and Film screen (src), a helper that holds your film source. Running it again updates them; nothing else is changed.

3 · Every stream

Switch to the DinkDonk scenes when you're on break or before the film. The control panel moves the camera, runs the auditorium phases (seating › lights down › film › lights up) and the automatic director. It works from your phone too.

Film on the cinema screen: on OBS 32 and older the auditorium talks to OBS by itself. Newer OBS (33+) won't let a website inside OBS reach it, so then keep the control panel open in this browser on this PC during the film: it passes the screen position on to OBS. Its top line says whether that link is up.
